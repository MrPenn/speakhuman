National Election Day (Presidential): November 3

For the 2016 Presidential Election, the Current Population Survey (CPS), Voting and Registration supplement found 70.3% of voting-age citizens (population 224,059,000) were registered and 61.4% voted.

National STEM Day: November 8

The 2019 American Community Survey estimates the total population age 25+ with a Bachelor’s degree or higher at 75M: 26M are in science and engineering (35.4%).

National Aviation History Month: November
