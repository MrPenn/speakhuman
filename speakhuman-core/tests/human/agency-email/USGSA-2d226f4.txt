Subject: Rocky Mountain Region Overview of GSA Professional Services Contracts

When: Tuesday, May 11 from 10 - 11 a.m. MST
Where: Virtual/Online

This free training provides information on GSA professional services acquisition solutions, programs, and resources for federal customers of GSA’s Rocky Mountain Region, which supports agencies in Colorado, Montana, North Dakota, South Dakota, Utah, and Wyoming.

Discussion Topics:

Get an overview of GSA's Professional Services Schedule(PSS) and OASIS, as well as a comparison of the two for determining the best fit for your requirement.

Also included are:

market research tools,

Ordering Guides,

how to navigate Scope Reviews and

upcoming additions over the next year.

There is no charge for this event, and you can earn 1.5 Continuous Learning Points (CLP).
