Notice of Resource Center Operating Status

In response to the ongoing coronavirus pandemic, the U.S. Department of Labor’s (DOL) Resources Centers have temporarily moved to telephone contact only.

Through this period of nationwide challenge, we remain fully operational and committed to ensuring that the needs of our claimants, authorized representatives, and stakeholders are met and that they continue to receive the assistance needed to allow for timely adjudication of their claims.

Resource Center staff will be available by telephone to answer questions, file claim forms, and conduct services necessary to continue operations. If you have documents that need transmitting to the Department of Labor, we strongly encourage you to use the Energy Document Portal (EDP) to upload documents directly to your case file.

Each Tuesday, a Resource Center staff member will be available from 8:30 a.m. – 5:00 p.m. to receive documents that require submission to the Department of Labor. If you have documents that you would like to send to the Department of Labor, you may leave them at the door during these times. In order to do so, please call the Resource Center when you arrive. The phone number for each Resource Center is listed below. Resource Center staff members will not be available to answer questions in person. NOTE: Please DO NOT leave any documents at Resource Center doors on days other than Tuesday and without confirming that the Resource Center staff person sees you leaving the documents. This is for the confidentiality of your personal and health information.

The Department of Labor will continue to monitor the situation and will open Resource Centers to visitors as soon as possible.
